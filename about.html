Who we are

A technical partner,
not a middleman

KAVITY is an engineering and supply company for specialised building systems, based in Amman and working in partnership with KABRI International Contracting. We are the exclusive agent in Jordan for UNITECH – IKK Group.

Our range covers mechanical cladding fixation, cable management and MEP supports, architectural expansion joints, raised access flooring, ceiling grid, entrance matting and wall protection, blockwork accessories and chutes — backed by more than fifteen years of experience in supply, execution and consultation.

Why it matters

Where your material
comes from changes everything

A reseller

Passes on whatever arrives

Gauge varies between shipments. A short delivery mid-job leaves the site waiting, with no one chasing the order at the factory. Two years later, the same item no longer matches.

KAVITY

Engineers it first

The system is sized from your drawings and load cases before a price is quoted, and the same team stays with it from the preliminary study through to completion on site.

Certifications

Manufactured to a certified system

Our cable management, support and fixation systems are manufactured by SFSP and UNITECH — an ISO 9001:2015 certified group with a design and engineering office in Stuttgart. Their cable trays and ladders are independently type-tested by DEKRA and carry the KEMA-KEUR mark.

  • ISO 9001:2015Quality management system — certified by Alcumus ISOQAR, UK
  • DEKRA · KEMA-KEURCable trays and ladders type-tested to EN 61537:2007 and IEC 61537:2023
  • Q-MarkProduct certification for SFSP-manufactured systems

  • NEMA VE 1 · VE 2Cable tray systems and installation
