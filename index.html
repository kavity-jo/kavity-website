Who we are

Engineered building systems, from design to site.

KAVITY is an engineering and supply company for specialised building systems, and the exclusive agent in Jordan for UNITECH – IKK Group. We work from the project drawings, specifications and load cases: our engineers select and size the system, prepare the calculations, shop drawings and submittals a consultant needs, and follow the order through manufacturing, delivery and site support.

  • Engineered per projectLoads, spans, fixings and movement checked against the drawings — not picked from a price list.
  • Submittal-readyShop drawings, data sheets, calculations and samples prepared for consultant approval.
  • One team, every systemFaçade fixation, cable management and MEP supports, expansion joints, raised floors, ceilings, protection profiles, blockwork accessories and chutes.
  • Stock in AmmanStandard brackets, channels, anchors and fixings held locally.
