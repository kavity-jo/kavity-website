Who we are

Façade fixation, engineered before it is supplied.

KAVITY is the exclusive UNITECH agent in Jordan. Design and structural calculation are handled in-house: our engineers work from the project drawings and load cases, size the fixation system and issue the calculations and shop drawings required for approval — then follow it through delivery and installation on site.
