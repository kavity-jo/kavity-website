FRP cable management

FRP & steel support systems

أنظمة الدعامات من FRP والفولاذ

FRP and steel strut channel, cantilever arms, U- and I-supports, plates and clamps for carrying FRP cable trays and ladders.

Request a technical proposal Download catalogue PDF · 11.3 MB
FRP trays & laddersFRP grating & handrailFRP & steel support systems

Pultruded FRP C-channel (FCH 220 and FCH 420) carries cable supports in corrosive and salt-laden environments, with stainless steel fittings. Steel framing channel, cantilever arms and slotted U- and I-supports with head plates complete the support system where steel is acceptable.

Load tables for the channels and cantilever arms are given in the catalogue for each span and arm length.
