Cable ladders

Fittings & supports

القطع الخاصة والدعامات لسلالم الكوابل

Bends, tees, risers, couplers, cantilever arms and trapeze framing sized to the ladder profile.

Request a technical proposal Download catalogue PDF · 8.4 MB
Welded cable laddersSwaged cable laddersFittings & supportsFirestop systems

Fittings are made to the same side rail height and width as the ladder, and supports are spaced from the published load table for the actual cable load. Cantilever arms and trapeze frames are built on channel, so levels can be adjusted on site.

Both welded and swaged rung connections are available. Welded ladders have C-channel rungs MIG-welded to the side rails; swaged ladders have tubular rungs cold-swaged into the rails, in aluminium or steel, with no welding heat to affect the temper and strength of the rail metal. Both come with published NEMA load classes.
