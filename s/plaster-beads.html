Plaster beads & expanded metal

Plaster beads

زوايا القصارة

Angle, stop, control joint, architrave and movement beads — the range that gives plaster and render straight, reinforced edges and controls cracking.

Request a technical proposal Download catalogue PDF · 7.9 MB
Plaster beadsExpanded metal & lathSteel lintelsBlockwork accessoriesBlockwork reinforcement

Beads are fixed before rendering and give the plasterer a true edge to work to. Angle beads protect external corners, stop beads finish the render at openings and other materials, and control joint beads divide large areas and allow movement, limiting shrinkage cracking. Stainless A4 beads are used externally and in wet areas.

Plaster beads, expanded metal lath, steel lintels, blockwork accessories and blockwork reinforcement are all covered by the same KAVITY-03 catalogue.
