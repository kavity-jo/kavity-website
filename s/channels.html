C-channel strut systems

Channels

قنوات C للتثبيت (سي تشانل)

Plain and slotted strut channel in 41 and 50 mm series, in standard thicknesses of 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5 mm, in single and back-to-back sections.

Request a technical proposal Download catalogue PDF · 8.9 MB
ChannelsToothed channelsFittingsAccessories & anchors

Choose the profile from the span and the load: the shallow 41 × 21 for light work close to the surface, 41 × 41 as the general standard, and back-to-back sections (41 × 82, 50 × 50) for longer spans and heavier equipment. Every channel takes a channel nut anywhere along its open slot; slotted-back channel can also be bolted through its back, while plain channel keeps the full section strength.

The full accessory range covers plain and angle fittings, gusseted brackets, base plates, threaded rod connectors, pipe clamps and beam clamps, all dimensionally matched to the channel profile.
