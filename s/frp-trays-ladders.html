FRP cable management

FRP trays & ladders

حوامل وسلالم الكوابل من FRP

Pultruded fibreglass trays and ladders for corrosive environments — lightweight, non-conductive and non-magnetic.

Request a technical proposal Download catalogue PDF · 11.3 MB
FRP trays & laddersFRP grating & handrailFRP & steel support systems

In chemical plants, desalination, water treatment and coastal sites, galvanised steel can corrode well within the design life of the building. Pultruded FRP trays and ladders are corrosion-resistant and need minimal maintenance on site, and vinyl ester resin is available for the most aggressive environments.

FRP is also non-conductive and non-magnetic, which removes the earthing and induction concerns of a metal system, and its high strength-to-weight ratio makes it easy to handle on long runs and rooftop plant.
