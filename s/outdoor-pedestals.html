Outdoor pedestals

القواعد الخارجية القابلة للتعديل (بيدستال)

Adjustable pedestals for terraces and roof decks — the slab below can fall to drain while the finish above stays level.

Request a technical proposal Download catalogue PDF · 17.5 MB
Outdoor pedestalsIndoor access floorsTimber decking supports

Each pedestal is set to its own height, so the finished surface stays level while the slab below falls to its drains. Water runs freely beneath the finish, the membrane is not penetrated, and any tile can be lifted to reach a drain.

Pedestals adjust from 10 mm to 945 mm and accept porcelain, granite, HPL, timber decking, carpet tile and stainless panels, with self-levelling heads for sloped slabs.
