MEP & cable management

Cable trays & accessories

حوامل الكوابل (كيبل تري) وملحقاتها

Perforated and solid cable trays with the full run of bends, tees, reducers and covers, manufactured to IEC 61537.

Request a technical proposal Download catalogue PDF · 5.0 MB

Trays are supplied in four duty classes — light, medium, heavy and very heavy — each in its own gauge (1.0, 1.2, 1.5 and 2.0 mm), so the section is matched to the cable load and the support spacing rather than over-specified across the whole building.

Every tray is backed by a published load table giving the safe working load at each span. The range covers horizontal and vertical bends, tees, crosses, reducers, covers, couplers, earth continuity straps and the full support range.
